## About me

Full-stack developer, freshly graduated with a **Higher Vocational Diploma in
Multiplatform Application Development** (Spain, 2026). Next year I start
**Web Application Development** to cover the other half.

Meanwhile I work as a **freelancer** on small and mid-sized projects: corporate
websites, management applications and custom tools. I care about work that
actually reaches production — domain, deployment, performance and SEO included,
not just `localhost:3000`.

- 🌱 Currently learning **serverless architectures** and **edge computing** with Cloudflare Workers
- 🛠️ I enjoy wrestling with awkward runtimes and shipping real apps to production
- 📍 Segovia, Spain — happy to work remotely
- 💬 Reach me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevin-prol-mozo/) or by [email](mailto:kevinprolmozo@gmail.com)
